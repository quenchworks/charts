Quenchworks step-ca is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

The PKI (root + intermediate CA, first provisioner, ca.json) was generated once
at install by the {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-init Job and lives on
the PVC {{ include "step-ca.pvcName" . }}. It is stable across restarts.

Reach the CA over HTTPS from inside the cluster:

  https://{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Port-forward it to your laptop:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 9000:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  # then: curl -k https://127.0.0.1:9000/health   -> {"status":"ok"}
  #       curl -sk https://127.0.0.1:9000/roots.pem   # the CA root certificate

The first provisioner is "{{ .Values.ca.provisioner }}". Its password and the CA
key password come from Secret {{ include "step-ca.secretName" . }}
({{ if .Values.auth.existingSecret }}your existingSecret{{ else }}chart-managed{{ end }}).
CHANGE THEM AFTER BOOTSTRAP and manage provisioners with `step ca provisioner`.

The image is pinned by digest and cosign-signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
