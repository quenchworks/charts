trust-manager {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Bundle sources (ConfigMaps and Secrets) are read from the trust namespace,
{{ .Release.Namespace }}. A Bundle that gathers a CA from there into every namespace:

  apiVersion: trust.cert-manager.io/v1alpha1
  kind: Bundle
  metadata:
    name: my-ca
  spec:
    sources:
      - configMap: {name: my-ca-source, key: ca.crt}
    target:
      configMap: {key: ca-bundle.crt}

  kubectl get bundle my-ca      # Synced=True once the ConfigMaps are written
