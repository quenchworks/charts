Host-based auth trusts the `crate` superuser from local connections only, and a
port-forward counts as local. Create an application user that way:

  kubectl port-forward {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-0 4200 &
  curl -s -H 'Content-Type: application/json' localhost:4200/_sql \
    -d '{"stmt":"CREATE USER app WITH (password = '\''change-me'\'')"}'
  curl -s -H 'Content-Type: application/json' localhost:4200/_sql -d '{"stmt":"GRANT ALL TO app"}'

Then connect over the PostgreSQL protocol as app with that password.
