Grav serves the site on {{ ternary "https" "http" (eq (include "quench-common.tls.enabled" .) "true") }}://{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}/ and the admin on /admin.

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Sign in to /admin as {{ .Values.admin.username }} (created on the first start):

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d

Plugins and themes come with the image; GPM installs into the read-only image are not possible.
