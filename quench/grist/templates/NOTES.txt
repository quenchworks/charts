Quenchworks Grist {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Reach it from inside the cluster:

  http://{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Port-forward it to your laptop:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 8484:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  # then open http://127.0.0.1:8484

With no login system configured, Grist signs every visitor in as
{{ .Values.adminEmail | default "you@example.com" }}. Keep the Service private, or set up OIDC, SAML or
forward auth (GRIST_OIDC_*, GRIST_SAML_*, GRIST_FORWARD_AUTH_* through extraEnvVars)
before exposing it.

Formulas run in the pyodide sandbox (Python in WebAssembly under deno), so the pod
needs no extra privileges. The home database and documents persist on the PVC at /persist.

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
