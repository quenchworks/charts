The croc relay is on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}:{{ .Values.relay.basePort }} (ports {{ include "croc.portList" . }}).

Relay password:

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "croc.secretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.password}' | base64 -d

Send and receive through it (croc 10 or newer, the code phrase in CROC_SECRET):

  CROC_RELAY=<relay-host>:{{ .Values.relay.basePort }} CROC_PASS=<password> CROC_SECRET=<code-phrase> croc send FILE
  CROC_RELAY=<relay-host>:{{ .Values.relay.basePort }} CROC_PASS=<password> CROC_SECRET=<code-phrase> croc
