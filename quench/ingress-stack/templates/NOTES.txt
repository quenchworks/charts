QuenchWorks ingress-stack is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

  IngressClass   nginx (cluster default)
  ClusterIssuer  ingress-ca, cert-manager's default; root CA in Secret ingress-root-ca

Give an Ingress a certificate from the stack's CA:

  metadata:
    annotations:
      kubernetes.io/tls-acme: "true"
  spec:
    tls:
      - hosts: [app.example.internal]
        secretName: app-tls

Clients trust the root:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret ingress-root-ca -o jsonpath='{.data.ca\.crt}' | base64 -d > ca.crt

ingress-ca is a private CA. For public certificates, add an ACME ClusterIssuer
and annotate Ingresses with cert-manager.io/cluster-issuer: <its name>.
