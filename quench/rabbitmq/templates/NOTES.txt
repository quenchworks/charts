Quenchworks RabbitMQ is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Connect from inside the cluster (AMQP 0-9-1 / 1.0):

  Host: {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local
  Port: {{ .Values.service.port }}
  User: {{ .Values.auth.username }}

Get the password:

  kubectl get secret --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "rabbitmq.secretName" . }} \
    -o jsonpath="{.data.{{ include "rabbitmq.passwordKey" . }}}" | base64 -d

Then connect with:

  amqp://{{ .Values.auth.username }}:<password>@{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}/

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
