sish accepts SSH on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}:2222 and serves tunnels over HTTP on :8080 as <name>.{{ .Values.domain }}.

From a machine with an authorized key:

  ssh -p 2222 -R app:80:localhost:3000 <sish address>

then open http://app.{{ .Values.domain }} (point *.{{ .Values.domain }} at the HTTP port).
