Quenchworks KubeRay is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}, watching {{ if .Values.watchNamespaces }}{{ join ", " .Values.watchNamespaces }}{{ else }}all namespaces{{ end }}.

It manages RayCluster, RayJob, RayService and RayCronJob resources. The CRDs are in
the chart's crds/ directory: Helm installs them once and never upgrades or deletes
them, so apply a newer chart's crds/ yourself when you upgrade across versions.

The Ray workloads themselves run the Ray image you set in each resource
(rayproject/ray or your own); this chart only runs the operator.

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify ghcr.io/quenchworks/images/kuberay \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
