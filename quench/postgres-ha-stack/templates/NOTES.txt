  postgres-ha-primary:5432   the current leader (read-write)

Superuser postgres, database {{ .Values.postgresql.auth.database }}. Password:

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} postgres-ha-auth -o jsonpath='{.data.postgres-password}' | base64 -d

Cluster state:

  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} postgres-ha-0 -c postgresql -- patronictl list
