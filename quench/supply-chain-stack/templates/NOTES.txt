QuenchWorks supply-chain-stack is installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Enforce signed QuenchWorks images in a namespace:

  kubectl label namespace my-app {{ .Values.verifyImages.namespaceLabel }}=true

There, Pods may only use {{ join ", " .Values.verifyImages.allowedImages }}, and every
QuenchWorks image must carry a cosign signature from a QuenchWorks build
workflow (checked against Sigstore). Kyverno pins each admitted image to its
digest. Other namespaces are not affected.

trivy-operator writes VulnerabilityReports next to each workload:

  kubectl get vulnerabilityreports -A
