The Mercure hub is on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}:{{ .Values.service.port }} at /.well-known/mercure.

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Your app signs publisher tokens with:

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "mercure.secretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.publisher-key}' | base64 -d

Tokens must be modern access tokens (typ at+jwt, iss one of {{ join ", " .Values.trustedIssuers }}, aud, exp, authorization_details): see the chart README.
