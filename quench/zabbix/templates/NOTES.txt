Quenchworks Zabbix is installed: the server, the web frontend and PostgreSQL.

Open the frontend:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-web {{ .Values.web.service.port }}:{{ .Values.web.service.port }}
  http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.web.service.port }}/

Log in as Admin with the password from the zabbix-db Secret:

  kubectl get secret zabbix-db -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d

Upstream's default Admin/zabbix does not work: the schema init replaced it.
Agents and senders reach the server at {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-server:10051.
