AdGuard Home is on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}: web UI on port {{ .Values.service.port }}, DNS on {{ .Values.service.dnsPort }} (UDP and TCP).

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 3000:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Sign in as "{{ .Values.admin.username }}"; its password:

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "adguardhome.secretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d

Point clients (or your network's DHCP) at the Service for DNS; use service.type LoadBalancer to reach it from outside the cluster.
