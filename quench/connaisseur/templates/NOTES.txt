Quenchworks Connaisseur is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Every Pod, Deployment, ReplicaSet, DaemonSet, StatefulSet, Job, CronJob and
ReplicationController created outside {{ prepend .Values.webhook.excludeNamespaces .Release.Namespace | uniq | join ", " }}
now goes through its admission webhook (failurePolicy {{ .Values.webhook.failurePolicy }}).
{{- if and (eq (len .Values.policy) 1) (eq (index .Values.policy 0).validator "allow") }}

The default policy admits every image. Add a cosign or notary validator and point the
policy at it to start enforcing signatures (see the chart README).
{{- end }}

The webhook certificate is generated by Helm and kept across upgrades
(Secret {{ include "connaisseur.tlsSecret" . }}).

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
