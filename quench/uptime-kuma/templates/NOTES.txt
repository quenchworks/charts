Quenchworks Uptime Kuma {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Reach the UI from inside the cluster:

  http://{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Port-forward it to your laptop:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 3001:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  # then open http://127.0.0.1:3001

The first visit creates the admin account. Do it right after installing: until then,
anyone who can reach the UI can claim it.

Monitors, history and settings (SQLite) persist on the PVC at /app/data.

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
