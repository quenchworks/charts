The CrowdSec LAPI listens on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-lapi.{{ .Release.Namespace }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}{{ if .Values.agent.enabled }}, fed by one agent per node{{ end }}.

Register a bouncer and point it at that URL:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-lapi -- cscli bouncers add my-bouncer
or keep the key in a Secret (BOUNCER_KEY_my-bouncer=<key>) and name it in lapi.envSecret.

List active decisions:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-lapi -- cscli decisions list
