Quenchworks Emissary-ingress is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}{{ if .Values.crds.enabled }}, with the
getambassador.io v3alpha1 CRDs{{ end }}.

Nothing listens until you add Listeners. A minimal HTTP setup:

  kubectl apply -f - <<YAML
  apiVersion: getambassador.io/v3alpha1
  kind: Listener
  metadata: {name: http, namespace: {{ .Release.Namespace }}}
  spec: {port: 8080, protocol: HTTP, securityModel: XFP, hostBinding: {namespace: {from: ALL}}}
  ---
  apiVersion: getambassador.io/v3alpha1
  kind: Mapping
  metadata: {name: my-app}
  spec: {hostname: "*", prefix: /my-app/, service: my-app.default:80}
  YAML

Gateway Service: {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} ({{ .Values.service.type }}, ports {{ .Values.service.httpPort }} and {{ .Values.service.httpsPort }}).
Diagnostics: kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}-admin 8877
  then http://127.0.0.1:8877/ambassador/v0/diag/

The images are pinned by digest and signed. Verify them:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
