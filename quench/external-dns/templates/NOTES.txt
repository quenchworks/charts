Watch it reconcile:

  kubectl logs -f deployment/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}

Give it something to publish -- an annotated Service:

  kubectl annotate svc <your-service> \
    external-dns.alpha.kubernetes.io/hostname=app.example.com

A controller with missing permissions still reports Ready while doing nothing, so
check the log for RBAC errors before you trust it:

  kubectl logs deployment/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} | grep -iE 'forbidden|cannot list'

Metrics and health are on container port 7979; the Service exposes them on
{{ .Values.service.port }}:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/healthz
  curl http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/metrics

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify ghcr.io/quenchworks/images/external-dns \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
