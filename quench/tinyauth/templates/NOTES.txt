Protect an app by pointing its ingress forward-auth at this Service: nginx
auth-url http://{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:{{ .Values.service.port }}/api/auth/nginx
with auth-signin {{ .Values.appUrl }}/login, or Traefik ForwardAuth .../api/auth/traefik.
Tinyauth's cookie covers the parent domain of the app URL, so protected apps live beside it.
