Quenchworks Blackbox Exporter is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

The exporter runs nonroot on container port {{ .Values.containerPort }}; the Service
exposes it on port {{ .Values.service.port }}:

  {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Probe a target over a port-forward:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/probe?target=https://example.com&module=http_2xx'

The response is Prometheus text; `probe_success 1` means the probe passed. The
exporter's own metrics (not probe results) are at /metrics, and /-/healthy answers
200 once the config has parsed.

this Service.
