  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl 'http://127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }}/probe?target=https://example.com&module=http_2xx'

exporter's own metrics (not probe results) are at /metrics, and /-/healthy answers
200 once the config has parsed.
