Quenchworks TigerBeetle is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} (single-node standalone).

TigerBeetle speaks its OWN binary protocol (NOT HTTP) on port {{ .Values.service.port }}. Connect with a
TigerBeetle client library or the `tigerbeetle` CLI — do not curl it.

In-cluster address:

  {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Reach it from your workstation with a port-forward:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.port }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Then point a TigerBeetle client at 127.0.0.1:{{ .Values.service.port }} (cluster id {{ .Values.cluster }}).

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
