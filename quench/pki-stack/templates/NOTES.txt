pki-stack is installed as release "{{ .Release.Name }}" in namespace "{{ .Release.Namespace }}".

Issue a certificate from your own CA:

  apiVersion: cert-manager.io/v1
  kind: Certificate
  metadata: {name: web, namespace: my-app}
  spec:
    secretName: web-tls
    dnsNames: [web.my-app.svc]
    issuerRef: {group: certmanager.step.sm, kind: StepClusterIssuer, name: {{ .Release.Name }}-step-ca}

Clients trust it through the root that trust-manager writes into every namespace:
ConfigMap {{ .Release.Name }}-root-ca, key {{ .Values.wiring.bundleKey }}.

  kubectl wait --for=condition=Ready stepclusterissuer/{{ .Release.Name }}-step-ca
  kubectl get bundle {{ .Release.Name }}-root-ca

The CA passwords are in Secret {{ index .Values "step-ca" "auth" "existingSecret" }}; the CA keys on the step-ca
PVC are encrypted with them, so back both up together.
