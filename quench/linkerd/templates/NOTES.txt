Linkerd edge-{{ .Chart.AppVersion }} runs in {{ .Release.Namespace }}: destination (with the policy server), identity and
the proxy injector, each with its own proxy.

Mesh a namespace (new pods get the proxy; restart existing ones):

  kubectl annotate namespace <ns> linkerd.io/inject=enabled
  kubectl -n <ns> rollout restart deploy

Check a meshed pod's proxy (tls="true" on its outbound requests means mutual TLS):

  kubectl get --raw /api/v1/namespaces/<ns>/pods/<pod>:4191/proxy/metrics | grep 'tls="true"'
