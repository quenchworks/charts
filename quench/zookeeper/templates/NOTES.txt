Quenchworks ZooKeeper is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Connect from inside the cluster:

  Client: {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc.cluster.local:{{ .Values.service.clientPort }}

Check health with the ruok four-letter word:

  kubectl run zk-check --rm -it --image=busybox --restart=Never -- \
    sh -c 'echo ruok | nc {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.service.clientPort }}'   # -> imok

The image is pinned by digest and signed. Verify it:

  cosign verify {{ .Values.image.repository }} \
    --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/quenchworks/.+' \
    --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
