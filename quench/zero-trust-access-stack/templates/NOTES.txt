Headscale and Authentik are starting in {{ .Release.Namespace }}. Headscale waits for Authentik.

  Authentik admin:  akadmin, password: kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret zt-secrets -o jsonpath='{.data.bootstrap-password}' | base64 -d
  Join a device:    tailscale up --login-server={{ tpl .Values.headscale.serverUrl . }}
                    then open the printed URL and sign in through Authentik.

Both URLs above are in-cluster names. For real devices set headscale.serverUrl and
headscale.oidcIssuer to addresses your devices and browsers can reach.
