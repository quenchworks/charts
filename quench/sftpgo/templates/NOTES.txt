SFTPGo is on {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}: web admin, web client and REST API on port {{ .Values.service.port }}, SFTP on {{ .Values.service.sftpPort }}.

  kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} svc/{{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}

Open /web/admin and sign in as "{{ .Values.admin.username }}"; its password:

  kubectl get secret -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} {{ include "sftpgo.secretName" . }} -o jsonpath='{.data.admin-password}' | base64 -d

Create users there; they sign in at /web/client or over SFTP.
