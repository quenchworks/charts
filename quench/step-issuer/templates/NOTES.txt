step-issuer {{ .Chart.AppVersion }} is installed as {{ include "quench-common.fullname" . }}.

Point a StepClusterIssuer at your step-ca and one of its JWK provisioners:

  apiVersion: certmanager.step.sm/v1beta1
  kind: StepClusterIssuer
  metadata:
    name: step-ca
  spec:
    url: https://step-ca.pki.svc
    caBundle: <base64 of the step-ca root certificate PEM>
    provisioner:
      name: <provisioner name>
      kid: <provisioner key id, from `step ca provisioner list`>
      passwordRef: {name: <secret>, namespace: <ns>, key: <key>}

Then reference it from a Certificate: issuerRef {group: certmanager.step.sm,
kind: StepClusterIssuer, name: step-ca}.
